## About

I am an Associate Professor in Industrial and Systems Engineering at Ferdowsi University of Mashhad. My work connects data-driven modeling, machine learning, optimization, and decision analytics to research, education, and real-world organizational problems.

I am particularly interested in building analytical methods in the area of marketing and CRM.

## Research interests

- **Data mining and machine learning:** predictive modeling, model evaluation, and learning from challenging datasets.
- **Customer and marketing analytics:** CRM, customer segmentation, churn and response modeling, customer lifetime value, and recommender systems.
- **Optimization and decision analytics:** multi-objective optimization and promotion/resource-allocation decisions.
- **Applied analytics for industrial and service systems:** translating operational and business data into actionable insight.
- **Reproducible computational research:** transparent experiments, documented workflows, and reusable research code.

## Teaching

I develop and teach data-focused material that connects concepts with executable examples, especially in data mining, machine learning, and Python-based analytics. My teaching approach emphasizes sound problem formulation, data understanding and quality, appropriate evaluation, and communicating analytical findings clearly.

## What I aim to share here

- Teaching notebooks, examples, and exercises for data mining and machine learning.
- Reusable Python workflows and experiment templates.
- Research code and supplementary material for projects that can be shared publicly.
- Applied analytics demonstrations using public or synthetic datasets.

For confidentiality and research-integrity reasons, client data, identifiable personal data, and unpublished or restricted material will not be posted here.

## Collaboration

I welcome research and teaching collaborations in machine learning, customer analytics, marketing analytics, optimization, and reproducible data science. The best starting point is to review the linked academic profiles and the repositories I publish here.
